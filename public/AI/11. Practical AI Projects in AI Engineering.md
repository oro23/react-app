# Practical AI Projects in AI Engineering

Practical AI projects are where AI engineering concepts become real applications. Instead of only training models, AI engineers typically **combine existing AI models, APIs, databases, retrieval systems, and software engineering practices** to build useful products.

Modern AI engineering projects usually involve:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- APIs
- Prompt engineering
- Vector databases
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Agents
- Cloud deployment
- User interfaces
- Monitoring and evaluation

------

# Key Takeaways

- Practical AI projects transform AI concepts into real applications.
- Modern AI engineers rarely build models from scratch; they build systems around powerful pretrained models.
- The most valuable projects combine **LLMs + APIs + RAG + vector databases + software engineering**.
- Building a portfolio of AI applications is one of the best ways to demonstrate AI engineering skills.
